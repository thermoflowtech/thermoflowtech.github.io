About Interliant

Interliant Technologies is based in Raleigh, North Carolina and was founded to translate advances in interfacial physics, surface science, and multiphase fluid dynamics into practical engineering mechanisms.

The company develops engineered interfaces that control how liquids nucleate, move, shed, and recycle on solid surfaces, with an intentional focus on early-stage mechanism validation before scale-up and commercialization.
