Technology Platform

Interliant’s core platform is built around engineered interfaces for deterministic control of liquid behavior. By designing how liquids interact with solid surfaces, we enable controlled transport, shedding, and phase-change dynamics that are difficult or impossible to achieve using conventional capillary-, gravity-, or pressure-driven approaches.

Core capabilities

  • Engineered interfacial wetting and low-hysteresis surface design
  • Surface-driven droplet transport, coalescence, and shedding
  • Control of condensation, evaporation, and phase-change processes
  • Multiphase-flow modeling and droplet-scale transport analysis
  • Benchtop experimentation and mechanism-level prototyping

Execution model

Interliant integrates modeling → surface design and fabrication → controlled experimentation → iterative refinement. This hybrid workflow enables rapid evaluation of surface-enabled mechanisms, identification of dominant physical limits, and early technical risk reduction before larger-scale system development.
